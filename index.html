ABOUT THE SERIES

Where coursework becomes scholarship

C.A.S.E. — the Class Assignment Series · Edition — is a bi-monthly student-edition scholarly series published by the Centre for Fintech & Strategic Business Research. It captures outstanding end-of-semester work that would otherwise live and die inside a graded folder, and gives it a permanent, citable home.

Every accepted piece passes faculty-led editorial screening, is paired with an ORCID iD, registered with a permanent Crossref DOI under prefix 10.67226, and hosted open access on PubPub under a CC BY-NC 4.0 license. Students leave each issue with a citable artifact for a CV, a graduate application, or a personal portfolio.

PUBLISHING WORKFLOW

From classroom to Crossref

  1. 01

    Editorial Screening

    Submissions on the official template are screened by the faculty-led editorial board on a rolling basis.

  2. 02

    Metadata Collection

    Title, authors, ORCID iDs, abstract, keywords, and references are structured for registration.

  3. 03

    Central Audit

    CFSBR validates metadata, builds the deposit package, and checks template and licensing compliance.

  4. 04

    Crossref Deposit

    DOIs are minted under prefix 10.67226 and the record goes live on the PubPub archive.

  5. 05

    Indexing & Discovery

    Records propagate to Google Scholar, OAI-PMH harvesters, and the author's ORCID profile.
