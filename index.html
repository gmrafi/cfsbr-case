About the series

A serious publication home for work that would otherwise disappear after grading.

C.A.S.E. is designed to move exceptional undergraduate assignments out of the temporary classroom cycle and into the formal scholarly record. Instead of ending as graded documents, selected works are reframed as citable case-based contributions with visible authorship and public archival presence.

The result is a student-edition publication layer that supports research identity, professional portfolios, graduate applications, and long-term discoverability.
