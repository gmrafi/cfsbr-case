Why C.A.S.E.

A scholarly home for exceptional undergraduate work.

In most academic cycles, strong assignments disappear after grading. C.A.S.E. gives selected coursework permanence, authorship visibility, and a formal route into the scholarly record.

The series reframes assignments as citable case-based contributions rather than temporary classroom documents, aligning student effort with editorial standards and long-term discoverability.

Publication flow Classroom to archive
  1. Outstanding coursework

    High-impact classroom work is nominated or prepared for submission.

  2. Editorial screening

    Formatting, scope, and academic integrity checks are completed.

  3. DOI-backed publication

    Accepted work enters a permanent scholarly record and public archive.
