About the Series

Where coursework becomes a permanent record

C.A.S.E. is a bi-monthly scholarly series designed to publish outstanding undergraduate assignments that are too good to disappear after grading. It turns classroom work into lasting academic output with editorial screening, structured metadata, and public discoverability.

Every accepted piece is prepared with author information, ORCID support, and references, assigned a Crossref DOI under prefix 10.67226, and archived on PubPub under a CC BY-NC 4.0 license. Students leave with a citable artifact for portfolios, scholarships, graduate admissions, and future research trajectories.

Scope of the Series

What we publish

C.A.S.E. focuses on high-quality undergraduate work that is evidence-driven, clearly structured, and useful beyond the classroom. The submission language can evolve later — the design system and section grammar come first.

Structured assignment outputs that combine business logic, methods, discussion, and recommendations for real-world stakeholders.

How it works

The C.A.S.E. workflow

Frontend clarity, centralised metadata, DOI registration, and long-term discoverability — using the same structural publishing logic that already makes the broader CFSBR ecosystem look premium and credible.

01

Internal Screening

Faculty and editorial review determine whether the assignment is publication-worthy and template-compliant.

02

Frontend Preparation

The issue landing page and publication surfaces are assembled on the C.A.S.E. site before archive release.

03

Metadata Collection

Title, authors, abstract, references, ORCID, and thematic tags are packaged in a structured workflow.

04

Central Audit

CFSBR validates metadata, checks licensing, and prepares the Crossref deposit and archive-ready record.

05

DOI Activation

The paper goes live with a permanent DOI, PubPub archive record, and indexing-ready metadata footprint.
