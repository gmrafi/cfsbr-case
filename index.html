Why we exist

A scholarly home for outstanding undergraduate work.

In conventional academic cycles, thousands of strong student assignments disappear after grading. C.A.S.E. changes that pattern by giving outstanding coursework permanence, authorship visibility, and a credible route into the scholarly record.

The series frames assignments as citable case-based contributions rather than temporary classroom documents, aligning student effort with publication standards and professional discoverability.
